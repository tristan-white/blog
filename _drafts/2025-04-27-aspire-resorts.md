Last Fall I surveyed my credit card benefits to figure out if any of the perks would expire at the end of the year. I came to realize that I had a lot of use-or-lose benefits that could nearly fund an entire trip to the Portuguese Azores, so I flew there and stayed for five nights.

<!-- <img src="/assets/images/azores/07.JPG" style="max-width: 50%" class=".right"> -->

That trip opened my eyes to the full value of the [American Express Hilton Aspire Card](https://higheraspire.com/about).

When I arrived in the Azores, I didn't have two of my five nights booked for a stay anywhere. I started reviewing each credit card I had to ensure I hadn't missed any perks that would give me a free night anywhere, and lo and behold - I learned the Aspire Card gives members **$200** towards *eligible Hilton Resorts* every six months.

By chance, I happened to be near the [DoubleTree by Hilton Lagoa Azores](https://higheraspire.com/hotel/PDLLADI). It was the off season for the Azores, so I was able to cover almost **two nights** with the Aspire Card **$200 Credit**. Since being a holder of the Aspire Card also gave me **Diamond status**, I was given a free breakfast each morning and an upgrade to an ocean view King room.

## The Problem

I was impressed with the $200 credit benefit on the Aspire Card - two nights at a nice resort!

But would I always be able to find an accessible Hilton resort for a low price that was eligible for the Aspire credit? In other words:

*How hard is it to find nearby, low-priced, Hilton Aspire Resorts?*

Answer: More difficult than you'd expect.

If you're like me, using the Aspire credit to get $200 off a stay isn't very appealing if the stay already costs hundreds of dollars.
